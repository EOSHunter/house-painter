# Code of conduct

House Painter is for anyone who wants to plan a house, and everyone who wants to help with it. We want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, share houses and send changes.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Blender, to code, or to building and painting houses.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Keep feedback on a change about the change.
- Don't harass anyone, and don't post other people's private information. This includes the plans of someone else's home, unless they've shared them.
- Keep it on topic and keep it kind: no discrimination, personal attacks, sexual content or threats.

## What happens if it isn't followed

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and changes that break these rules, and may ask someone to stop or block them from the project. Decisions are made fairly and kept private where possible.

## Reporting a problem

Contact a maintainer privately, either through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile or by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/EOSHunter/house-painter/security/advisories/new) and saying it's a conduct issue. Please include links and what happened. Reports are taken seriously and handled with care.

This is a short, plain-language code of conduct. The [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is a fuller model, and its ideas inform this one.
